# Get started with Bindplane (Dynatrace Classic)

* Overview
* Published Sep 30, 2026

Find the information you need to connect Bindplane to Dynatrace and get your telemetry pipeline running.

* [**Install the Dynatrace Bindplane OTel Collector**﻿](https://docs.bindplane.com/readme/install-your-first-collector)

  Deploy the Dynatrace Bindplane OTel Collector on your hosts to start collecting telemetry.
* [**Add Dynatrace as a Bindplane destination**﻿](https://docs.bindplane.com/integrations/destinations/dynatrace)

  Add Dynatrace as a destination to Bindplane and send metrics, logs, and traces to your Dynatrace environment via OTLP.
