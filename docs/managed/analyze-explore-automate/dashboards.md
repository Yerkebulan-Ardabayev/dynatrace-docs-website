# Dashboards

# Dashboards

* 1-min read
* Updated on Oct 05, 2026

Each dashboard consists of tiles and visualizations that you can select, configure, and position to meet your needs. You can make different dashboards for specific people, roles, and teams, so everyone can focus on what's important to them. You can also drill down from a tile to investigate the monitored entities it reports on.

The pages in this section start with building dashboards and then cover sharing and organizing them. They end with concepts and reference pages for timeframes, settings, tiles, and visualizations.
