Before you can control or optimize cost, you need to see it. This subsection covers every way Dynatrace lets you view and export your consumption data.

## Overview

Dynatrace exposes the same underlying cost and usage data through several surfaces. Account Management gives you hourly totals, capability and environment breakdowns, and configurable cost-management views. The daily billing report shows costs by booking date. The Account Management API exposes the same data programmatically for finance integrations.
